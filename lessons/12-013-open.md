### 12.13 Open

1. The dominance prune is not sound. Witness: with the prune always on (z11k), grammar
   `S <- R0; R0 <- ((('c' R0) / 'c'*) 'a' !(&R0)); ...` on `acaab` loses the cost-3 repair at
   level 3; what remains is insertions of 'c' or 'a' at the end of the input, which never
   complete and double at every level (688,128 repairs at level 17). z11 turns the prune on
   only past 64(n+1) parses and no fuzzer case reached that point with the optimum pruned, but
   nothing bounds the search once it does. A sound bound (for example, the cost of deleting
   the whole input and inserting a shortest sentence) would at least end it.
2. Dense errors still cost about k^2.5 in z9's search (12.11 item 1).
3. z9's answer depends on the order in which budgets are tried (12.10 item 2).
4. `_build` and `walk` overflow on trees deeper than about 3,000 levels.
