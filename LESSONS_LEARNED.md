Seventy-odd engines across six architectural lines were built, measured, and
archived to reach the c-series: one algebra refined engine by engine, ending
at c14 — c13's judgment (c12's substitution breakthrough, the first to beat
the long c6–c10 judgment plateau, plus c13's one rule) on a machine whose
recursion depth and per-end scans are bounded by the grammar rather than by
the document, so a 100,000-character input is read in about two seconds
where c13 overflowed the stack. This file
records the yardstick, the standing results, the critical
lessons, and the refutations that must not be retried. (A refutation is a
measurement under a stated protocol, and one of them was itself wrong:
lesson 46 refutes the retained-width bound of lesson 33. A retry under a
different premise or protocol is a new experiment, not a repeat.) The
c1–c19 comparison of 2026-09-06 is in §2 beside the standing table; the
c18/c19 round and its audit are lessons 44–55. Everything else — the
era-1/era-2 history (insights I1–I107 in long form), the last full
twelve-engine table, and the archived lines' detailed accounts — is preserved
in `dart/experiments/recovery/attic/OLD_LESSONS_LEARNED.md`.

## 3. The critical lessons

The numbered lessons on architecture, ranking, representation, method and the machine, then one entry per round from c14 to x and z (rounds of 2026-09-02 to 2026-10-23). Windows, beams, packed forests and regular bounds each fix one measure, but none gives accuracy, small size, short-input speed and many-error scaling together. The cdx and cl lines (cross-review rounds 1-25) cut invalid trees with guards, most of which each decide some fuzzer case. The pd and u lines search PEG derivatives, so every tree is valid by construction, at about n^3 on extra brackets. The sk line shows light skip-and-insert recovery reaching 0.963 at under 460 LOC. The x line is small but repeats memo work. The z line (credit-settled memoized recovery on the native parser) reaches battery 0.9904 by z5-z7.

- [The architecture — three discoveries that define the design](lessons/03-001-the-architecture-three-discoveries-that-define-the.md): Three discoveries define the design: the LR grow-loop is the recovery loop (I100), so left recursion is recovery at cost zero; parsing mode is budget zero (I101) and the budget is the horizon (A3), and deleting the budget ladder cost about 100x latency twice; judgment must be whole-document and simultaneous, because the b-line's greedy commit-then-resume has a proven 0.88 ceiling.
- [The judgment — what a reading is worth](lessons/03-002-the-judgment-what-a-reading-is-worth.md): Lessons 4-9 on ranking: five keys, each placed by a failing case (I105, I111), with ablations (dropping latest doubt -3.4 perfect, the stranded count -2.6); the swallow is derived, never stored (I108); the boundary claim is charged once because the budget is an additive ledger (I94, I110); the five counters are the floor (I111); the latest same-price rival wins ties; names are evidence (I81/I96).
- [The representation — how the search stays cheap and honest](lessons/03-003-the-representation-how-the-search-stays-cheap.md): Lessons 10-14 on representation: the way is a cons cell whose scalars are additive caches or derived at use (I109, "derive, don't account"); the way-front is the memo cell and insertion is the improvement test (I103); eager trees cost 20-31%, so only winners are built (I88); folding the parser into node classes cut latency 20% (c6); one versioned left-recursion law with a one-boolean involved set serves both fibers (I112, c7).
- [The method — how the lessons were won](lessons/03-004-the-method-how-the-lessons-were-won.md): Lessons 15-17 on method: the battery is a design object to curate in the open (I107); keep checks the battery cannot see and confirm each fails on a known-bad engine (freespan's probe list was once empty, so its passes were vacuous); compute the Pareto frontier with pareto.py rather than by eye, and record the number that keeps or removed each mechanism.
- [The machine — what the c9 round measured](lessons/03-005-the-machine-what-the-c9-round-measured.md): Lessons 18-21 from the c9 round: measure data shape first (71% of cell consults see one reading), which turned a map into a list and gave 1.7x while unmeasured per-operation changes were noise (I115); under Dart's GC, avoiding allocation usually loses (I116); a ratio is comparable only within its session; a blended ratio hid c10's 2.4-2.6x clean-input cost.
- [The repair — what a substitution must prove (the c12 round)](lessons/03-006-the-repair-what-a-substitution-must-prove.md): Lessons 22-23 from c12: unconditional substitution was the entire damaged-latency cost, because each swap resumes at a position no clean reading visits and cascades memo misses. The _resumes condition (I121) requires the rest of the sequence to read cleanly after the swap: -27% then calls, battery bit-identical, damaged 1.06x to 0.98x c9. Where a repair is offered sets its cost (1.198x to 0.98x).
- [The machine, second look — what the c14 round measured](lessons/03-007-the-machine-second-look-what-the-c14.md): Lessons 24-27 from c14: the repetition sweep asked steps at the round's whole budget, not the unspent remainder; the battery could not see it, but recursion depth grew with input (1,159 frames). The handoff cut 6k/4 from 18.6 s to 0.7 s. _Front scans up to 32 entries then indexes (3.7x at 24k/4); recovery cost scales with the error's position; locality was refuted a third time.
- [The machine, third look — what the c15 round measured (2026-09-03)](lessons/03-008-the-machine-third-look-what-the-c15.md): c15 (1,018 LOC) runs c14's judgment as a graph-structured stack with a beam of W origins per (position, shape): the exact optimum needs Θ(n²) walker summaries, and W=8 loses nothing on the battery (W=1: 0.9290). Keying frames by whole stack is exponential, by nothing unsound; replay on every link closed a 0.9834 gap. c15 is linear at fixed budget but 1.5-4x slower than c14 below 10k characters; memory is open.
- [The machine, fourth look — what the c16 round measured (2026-09-04)](lessons/03-009-the-machine-fourth-look-what-the-c16.md): c16 (1,015 LOC, +25.8%) puts c15's beam into c14's cells at both offer sites and memoizes plain matches, which were the hidden quadratic tail: battery bit-identical, 1.38x c14 instead of 3.75x, 150k characters in 15.5 s. Exact refills are correct but not worth it. The claimed O(|G|·W·n) bound is refuted by lesson 46; linear growth is only observed, and readings remain the memory.
- [The machine, fifth look — what the c17 round measured (2026-09-04)](lessons/03-010-the-machine-fifth-look-what-the-c17.md): c17 (1,330 LOC) tries repairs only inside windows over where readings died, walked per frame two evidenced units back, re-running a budget when a new death appears: 200k characters in 5.36 s / 1.53 GB against c16's 146.9 s, battery 0.9900/86.0. A plain parse failing at budget zero must record a death. On short inputs the re-runs are the latency (2.3x c14), and the end-of-input re-run is required.
